Every fortnight's task is released at the last minute, per [course
policy](/policies/) — Parkinson's law, not an oversight.

## The brief

> Every second week, bring something. It does not have to be right.

The first tutorial lands in week 2 and repeats every fortnight after that for
the rest of the semester — this page stands in for the whole recurring series,
not just its first date.

## What you submit

Whatever the fortnight's lab task asks for, in whatever state it's in by the
tutorial. There is no format beyond "something a marker can look at and judge
against the split above."
