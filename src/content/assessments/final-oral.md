Same as the midsem: the exact angle is decided and released at the last
minute, per [course policy](/policies/).

## The brief

> Say something for ten minutes about the second half of this course, and
> mean at least one sentence of it.

Same room, same format as the midsem, different stakes: by now the course has
asked you to make one genuine attempt, and the final oral is where that
attempt has to hold up next to everything that came before it.

## What you submit

Nothing in advance, same as the midsem. What's different is what you're
expected to have: an answer you can actually stand behind, alongside
everything you learned to fake.
