## The brief

> Say something for ten minutes about the first half of this course, and
> keep saying it while someone tries to find the seam.

The midsem oral is the first real test of whether "confident delivery" is a
skill you have or a bit you were doing. There's no brief to point at and no
document to hand in — the exam is the thing you say, live, in the room.

## What you submit

Nothing in advance. You show up and talk. Bring whatever mental scaffolding
gets you through ten minutes of direct questioning; nothing physical is
checked at the door.

This is marked holistically — see above — because a rubric can't watch you
recover from a bad answer, only whether you did.
