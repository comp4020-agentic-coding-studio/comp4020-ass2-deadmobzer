Holds credentials that do not survive close inspection, which she
considers a proof of concept rather than a liability. Nobody has followed
up, which she also considers a proof of concept.

Teaches weeks 2, 3, 9 and 10: remote association, applying it live, the
philosophy of critique, and the production of pseudo-profundity. Her
reasoning: nobody is better placed to teach students how to make something
up than someone who has been doing it, uncaught, for her entire career.

Reachable by email between sessions; escalate to the convenor for anything
that's a course-level policy question rather than a mark on specific work.
