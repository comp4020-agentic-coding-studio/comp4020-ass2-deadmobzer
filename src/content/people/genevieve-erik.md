Gen Erik — the name she introduces herself with, title or no title — is the
founding convenor of SLOP1669. She founded the course after a long run of
convening others, and named this one after herself because nobody stopped
her.

Delivers weeks 1, 6 and 12 personally: the opening demonstration, the
midsemester revision, and the closing exam revision. A convenor who shows
up at the start, middle and end has, by her own accounting, technically
taught the whole course.

Outside of that: cats, coffee, and a long walk on the beach, roughly in
that order of priority. Ask about any of the three during consultation
hours and the session will run long.

Consultation hours are posted at the start of semester and are, notably,
not released at the last minute; that policy is reserved for briefs, not
for office hours.
