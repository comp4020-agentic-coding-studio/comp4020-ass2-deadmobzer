Hired as a one-off substitute when a lecturer called in sick during week 4
of a previous offering, and never formally uninvited. Colleagues started
calling him Justin Khayse on the basis that he was brought in "just in
case," and it stuck.

Teaches weeks 4, 5 and 11: the toolbelt survey, the live-fire last-minute
workflow, and the one week students are asked to do the assignment
properly, which he treats as an extended apology for the other two.

Reachable by email between sessions; escalate to the convenor for anything
that's a course-level policy question rather than a mark on specific work.
