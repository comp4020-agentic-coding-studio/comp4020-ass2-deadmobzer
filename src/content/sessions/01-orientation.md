The lecture demonstrated it; this session is where you try it yourself,
badly, in front of people who will forget by next week. Nothing here is
graded — the first fortnightly checkpoint is next week, and this is the
rehearsal.

## In the session

- pairs take turns bullshitting a topic pulled at random, ninety seconds
  each
- the room calls out the exact sentence where the bullshit started working
- a short debrief on what "confident" actually sounded like versus what
  felt confident from the inside

## Afterwards

Nothing is due. What you leave with is a rough sense of your own tell —
useful going into a semester where you'll be asked to hide it.
