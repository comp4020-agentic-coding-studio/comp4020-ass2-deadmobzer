No submission this week. The midsem is behind you and the satire pivot from
lecture needs a target before it needs a punchline — this session is where
that target gets picked and pressure-tested.

## In the session

- everyone pitches a candidate target, thirty seconds each
- the room votes on which targets are worth a whole piece versus a single
  joke
- notes on the difference between a target that's funny and one that's
  worth saying something about

## Afterwards

Nothing is due. Whatever target survives this session is what week 8's
tutorial builds on.
