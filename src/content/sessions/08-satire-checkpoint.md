The target from week 7's clinic gets its first real draft here. Bring the
piece, not the pitch — a target is easy to defend in the abstract and much
harder to defend once it's actually been written.

## In the session

- short demos of the finished pass, target named up front
- the room checks whether the joke survives without the delivery, and
  whether the commentary survives without the joke
- notes on what to cut before this counts as done

## Afterwards

Same split as always: half for showing up, half for whether the piece says
something rather than just landing.
