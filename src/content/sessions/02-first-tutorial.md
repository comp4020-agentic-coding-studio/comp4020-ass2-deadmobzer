The first tutorial that counts. Bring whatever you built from week 2's
abstraction lecture — the mark isn't for having the right answer, it's for
having built something specific enough to defend and vague enough to
survive questions.

## In the session

- short demos, one abstraction each, no slides
- the room tries to find the specific claim hiding under the vague one
- feedback aimed at where the abstraction is doing too much work

## Afterwards

This is the fortnight's mark: half attendance, half whether what you brought
was bullshitted with conviction rather than left blank.
