Same checkpoint format as weeks 2 and 4, with one addition: whatever you
bring gets a question put to it on the spot, because that's exactly what
the midsem oral does next.

## In the session

- short demos of the fortnight's task, same as always
- one direct question per person, answered without notes
- a debrief on what held up under questioning and what didn't

## Afterwards

The usual split for the fortnight's mark, plus whatever you learn here about
answering under pressure is the only revision the midsem gets.
