Mednick's associative theory of creativity treats a good idea as two distant
ideas dragged next to each other until they look related. That is also the
whole mechanism of a strong bullshit answer, so this week takes it as theory
before next week runs it live on an actual object.

## Outline

- abstraction as a hiding place, not a virtue, and how to tell the difference
  from the audience's seat
- remote association, in Mednick's sense, as the trainable skill underneath
  "it's more of a vibe"
- the first fortnightly tutorial's shape, and what "bullshitting" is being
  assessed alongside attendance
- reading before next week: whatever you can find that sounds load-bearing
