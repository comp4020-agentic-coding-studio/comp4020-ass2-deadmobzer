Ten weeks of bullshitting technique were never the point on their own; they
were rehearsal for recognising the difference when it counts. This week is
where that difference counts: an honest attempt at the assignment, using
whatever the semester actually taught you.

## Outline

- what changes when the brief is answered instead of gestured at
- which of the last ten weeks' techniques survive contact with a genuine
  attempt, and which turn out to have only ever worked as bits
- getting feedback on something real, which lands differently than feedback
  on something you never believed in
- what "revision" means with one week left and nothing left to fake
