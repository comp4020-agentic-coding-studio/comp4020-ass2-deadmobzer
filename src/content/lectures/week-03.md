Theory without demonstration is just a claim, and claims are cheap. This week
takes the abstraction toolkit from week 2 and runs it against a real problem,
badly, on purpose, so you can see exactly where the vagueness is doing the
work.

## Outline

- picking a problem specific enough to sound serious, vague enough to survive
  questions
- narrating the abstraction as it happens, not after
- the tell that separates a bullshitted answer from an honest "I don't know"
- setting up next week's pivot to tools and resources
