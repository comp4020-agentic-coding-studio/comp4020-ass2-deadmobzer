Revision, here, means rehearsing the performance rather than re-learning the
material — the midsem is oral, and an oral exam marks what you can say under
pressure, not what you could look up given time.

## Outline

- what the midsem oral is actually testing, versus what the syllabus implies
  it's testing
- rehearsing answers you don't fully believe until they sound like ones you
  do
- handling a question you have no answer to, out loud, without going silent
- what to bring, and what not to bring, into the room
