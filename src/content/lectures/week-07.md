Satire is bullshitting with a thesis: it still isn't true, but it's supposed
to mean something now. This week is the pivot from "convincing" to
"convincing and pointed" — the same skills, aimed somewhere.

## Outline

- the difference between a joke and a commentary, and why the assignment
  brief only rewards one of them
- picking a target worth aiming the last five weeks of skills at
- turning something mundane — a course, a form, a policy — into a
  commentary on the systems that produced it
- why this needs a part two next week
