Marilyn Strathern's line on audit culture is the target this half of the
course aims at: "when a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good
measure." A rubric is a measure that announced itself as a target from the
start, which makes it unusually easy to read as a text — this week is about
finding that reading, not yet delivering it.

## Outline

- the difference between a joke and a commentary, and why the assignment
  brief only rewards one of them
- picking a target worth aiming the last five weeks of skills at
- turning something mundane — a course, a form, a rubric — into a
  commentary on the systems that produced it
- why this needs a part two next week
