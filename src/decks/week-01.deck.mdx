# Live Bullshitting

A Demonstration Performed Without Preparation

Week 1 · no readings · no rehearsal

---

## Ground rules for the next fifty minutes

- nothing on these slides was checked before you walked in
- the convenor is going to bullshit an entire topic, live, and you're going
  to watch it happen
- your job is not to believe it — your job is to spot the exact moment it
  starts working

---

{/* _class: quote */}

> Bullshit is produced with "indifference to how things really are."

— Harry Frankfurt, *On Bullshit* (2005)

---

## The distinction that matters

- a **liar** cares about the truth — enough to carefully avoid it
- a **bullshitter** has stopped caring whether a claim is true at all
- only one of those is what this course teaches, and it isn't lying

---

## What you're marking, silently, while this happens

- confident delivery, holding under a follow-up question
- a claim that sounds specific but resolves to nothing checkable
- the exact sentence where the room stopped noticing
